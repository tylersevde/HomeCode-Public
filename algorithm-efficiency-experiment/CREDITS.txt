EVIDENCE AND CREDIT FOR RESULTS

The archive's original CPU measurements, the later Pi numerical experiments,
and the NPU language tasks are distinct measurements. Improvement against an
old GPU kernel is credited to that comparison; it is not presented as beating
the CPU. A CPU fallback answer is credited to the fallback, not to the model.
An answer that merely completed is not credited as strictly correct.

The final roadmap's correct conclusion is that no new policy passed every
promotion rule. The favorable CPU batch endpoint and improved GPU kernel remain
reported findings. The NPU revision missed its fixed accuracy gate. Stages that
never opened remain explicitly unmeasured. Implementation and checkpoint test
counts are credited separately from hardware evidence and actual restoration.
